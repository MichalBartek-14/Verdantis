Current conditions & vegetation alerts

Everything that isn't the multi-year historical pilot deliverable lives here: the latest weekly conditions snapshot, a scrubbable month-by-month view of the last year, and structural-break detection over the raw (uncomposited) NDVI series. All three are data feeds, not finished alert products - the numbers below are published by hand whenever publish_site.py is re-run after a fresh local pass, not fetched live from the backend.

Vegetation break alerts

A separate branch from the moisture monitoring above: every available raw Sentinel-2 scene over a chosen bounding box (no compositing), decomposed with STL (trend + season + remainder) and searched for changepoints with ruptures' PELT - the same conceptual approach BFAST (Verbesselt et al.) is built on, via GPU-free, pip-installable libraries. Treat detected breaks as candidates to investigate, not calibrated alerts.
