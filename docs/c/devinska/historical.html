The client-facing pilot deliverable

Up to five years of monthly Sentinel-2 median composites over the plot, reduced to three spectral indices server-side, then analysed locally: plot-average time series, a scrubbable month-by-month imagery view of the last year, and ERA5 temperature + precipitation overlays to sanity-check whether moisture drops track warm, dry spells.

Time series (plot-average, monthly)

NDVI tracks vegetation vigour; NDWI and NDMI are two moisture-proxy formulations of the same underlying signal (broad vs. narrow NIR band) - shown side by side rather than picked between, since they're correlated but not identical.

Monthly imagery, last year

Whole-plot NDVI/NDWI/NDMI composites, scrub month by month - the same 12-month window as the Sector Explorer's "Last year" option, shown here since it's a single time series rather than a per-sector breakdown.

Moisture vs. weather

NDWI merged against ERA5-based (Open-Meteo archive, no API key needed) monthly mean air temperature and total precipitation, for the same window and location.

Every threshold and correlation on this page is a pilot starting point. NDWI/NDMI "dry" thresholds (0.10 here) and the cloud-mask kernel sizes are copied from documented defaults, not independently tuned for this plot's species or soil - see the project README before using any number to drive a real decision.
