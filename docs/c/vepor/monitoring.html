Vegetation break alerts

A separate branch from the moisture monitoring above: every available raw Sentinel-2 scene over a chosen bounding box (no compositing), deseasonalized against a fixed harmonic model of the normal Central-European vegetation year (so ordinary spring green-up and autumn senescence aren't mistaken for a break) and searched for changepoints in what's left with ruptures' PELT - the same conceptual approach BFAST (Verbesselt et al.) is built on, via GPU-free, pip-installable libraries. Treat detected breaks as candidates worth a field check, not confirmed events or calibrated alerts.
