Verdantis turns free Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery into a plot-level view of vegetation vigour (NDVI), canopy/soil moisture (NDWI & NDMI), and structural change - built as a client-facing pilot for a single forestry plot, reusable for any shapefile.

Four views into the same plot

Each product below is generated locally from the same Sentinel-2 pipeline and openly documented - none of the thresholds are calibrated operational alerts yet, they're pilot starting points meant to be checked against field observations before driving real decisions.

📈

Historical Pilot

Up to 5 years of monthly NDVI/NDWI/NDMI time series, a frequency + magnitude "where does the plot lose water most" map, and ERA5 temperature + precipitation overlays.

Explore the deliverable → 🗺️

Sector Explorer

Interactive map - the plot split into 5×5-pixel sectors, toggle NDWI/NDMI, click any sector for its own moisture-vs-temperature history.

Monitoring & Alerts

Current weekly NDVI/NDWI/NDMI conditions, a scrubbable monthly imagery view of the last year, and BFAST-style structural break detection - harvest, dieback, or land-use change flagged in the raw NDVI series.
