The client-facing pilot deliverable

Up to five years of monthly Sentinel-2 median composites over the plot, reduced to three spectral indices server-side, then analysed locally: plot-average time series, a scrubbable month-by-month imagery view of the last two years, and ERA5 temperature + precipitation overlays to sanity-check whether moisture drops track warm, dry spells.

Time series (plot-average, monthly)

NDVI tracks vegetation vigour; NDWI tracks canopy/soil moisture. A second moisture formulation, NDMI, is computed too (see the Sector Explorer) but dropped here as redundant with NDWI - the two are highly correlated.

Monthly imagery, last 2 years

Whole-plot NDVI/NDWI composites, scrub month by month over a 2-year window - long enough to compare the same season (e.g. September) across consecutive years, not just see the latest one. NDMI is left out here as redundant with NDWI (see the Sector Explorer for both side by side).

Moisture vs. weather

NDWI merged against ERA5-based (Open-Meteo archive, no API key needed) monthly mean air temperature and total precipitation, for the same window and location.

Every threshold and correlation on this page is a pilot starting point. NDWI/NDMI "dry" thresholds (0.10 here) and the cloud-mask kernel sizes are copied from documented defaults, not independently tuned for this plot's species or soil - see the project README before using any number to drive a real decision.
