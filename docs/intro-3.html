From signal to warning

Tracked continuously across an entire plot, the vigour and moisture signals from page two stop being curiosities and start being an early-warning system.

🌵

Drought

Canopy and soil moisture (NDWI/NDMI) start dropping weeks before a tree visibly wilts. Cross-checked against ERA5 temperature and rainfall, that trend tells you whether a dry spell is driving it - or whether something else is.

🌊

Flood & waterlogging

A sudden shift in the moisture indices, localised to specific sectors of the plot, pinpoints where standing water or saturated ground is a problem - without walking the whole property to find it.

⛈️

Storms & severe weather

Structural break detection in the vigour record (BFAST-style) flags a real, lasting change - windthrow, hail damage, dieback - and tells it apart from ordinary seasonal swings in the same series.

Everything on the next pages - the Historical Pilot, the Sector Explorer, and Monitoring & Alerts - is built from exactly these signals, for your plot specifically.
