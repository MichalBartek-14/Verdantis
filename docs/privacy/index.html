Privacy note

What Verdantis collects about your plot and how it's used, in plain language. This is a pilot-stage draft, not a finished policy - ask us anything it doesn't cover.

What we collect

  • Your plot's boundary (the shapefile you provide) and the free Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery over it.
  • Vegetation and moisture indices (NDVI, NDWI, NDMI) and structural-change flags derived from that imagery.
  • If you've connected one, readings from your own field sensor(s) (temperature, position, soil moisture).
  • If you send a pilot enquiry, whatever you type into that form (name, email, plot location, message).

Why

Solely to run and improve your monitoring pilot, and to respond to enquiries about joining it. We don't sell, advertise against, or otherwise use this data for anything else.

Who can see it

Your dashboard is published at its own address and is meant to be access-restricted to you (Basic Auth today). Pipeline runs and data processing happen locally, by hand - there's no always-on server holding your data.

Enquiry form

Submitting the homepage's enquiry form opens a pre-filled email in your own mail client - the content is sent directly to us by email, not stored by this website itself.

Questions
