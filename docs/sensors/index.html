Two datasources, one picture

Verdantis combines two fundamental data sources: (A) satellite intelligence and (B) sensor ground-truth data. The two work in symbiosis, because of how differently each one scales. Satellite intelligence identifies the general pattern within a plot and helps you understand roughly where a problem sits - but it can't pin down an exact position, or an exact value.

This is where sensors come in. Sensors are purpose-built, metrologically calibrated devices capable of measuring the variables that actually drive canopy health on your plot - soil moisture, salinity, temperature, air conditions, and more. True values, in real time, with no delay.

Sensors become even more powerful combined with Sentinel-2 imagery. Sentinel-2 uncovers the general shape of a problem - drought, or one side of a plot losing moisture earlier than the rest. Once that pattern is identified, sensors can be deployed exactly where it's unfolding, pointing to the concrete crops or trees sitting on dry soil - no need to blanket-deploy across hectares of fields, just the specific locations that matter. That saves money, computing time, and work.
