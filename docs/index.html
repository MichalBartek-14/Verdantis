How it works

Verdantis pulls free Sentinel-2 satellite passes over your plot every few days and turns them into moisture and vegetation-vigour maps, building a multi-year history so dry spells, regrowth, and seasonal patterns show up as visible trends instead of one-off readings. An automated change-detection model continuously watches for abrupt structural breaks — clearings, storm damage, dieback — and flags them early, before they're obvious on the ground. Where a plot has live field sensors installed, their real-time ground readings fill the gaps between satellite passes, while the satellite view confirms whether a reading at one sensor holds true across the whole plot — each keeping the other honest. No satellites or hardware of ours required — just open Copernicus data, turned into something you can act on.
